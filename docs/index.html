<h2>About</h2>
<p>Hi! I am a <strong>Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong</strong>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/en/persons/cshswong/">Hau-San Wong</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.cs.cityu.edu.hk/~qzhan7/index.html">Qingfu Zhang</a>. I also work closely with Prof. <a href="https://weihuang05.github.io">Wei Huang</a> and Prof. <a href="https://andihan3.github.io/">Andi Han</a>.</p>
<p>My research studies the <strong>theory and algorithms of large-scale machine learning systems</strong>, including emergent in-context learning, agentic AI, LLM and diffusion post-training, reinforcement learning, non-log-concave sampling, and automated theorem proving. I am particularly interested in theory-grounded algorithm design with reproducible experiments.</p>
<p>I am currently a one-year research intern at <strong>CFAR, A*STAR</strong>, supervised by Prof. <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/atsushinitanda/home">Atsushi Nitanda</a>. Before that, I spent 2024–2025 with the <a href="https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/aip/generic_tech/deep_learn_theory/index.html">Deep Learning Theory Team at RIKEN AIP</a>, working under Prof. <a href="https://ibis.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/suzuki/">Taiji Suzuki</a>.</p>
<p>I received my B.S. in Mathematics from <strong>Xi'an Jiaotong University</strong> in 2023. In 2026, I also visited the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sydney, working with Prof. <a href="https://andihan3.github.io/">Andi Han</a>.</p>
